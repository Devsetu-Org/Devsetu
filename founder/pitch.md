DevSetu is online at https://devsetu.io. You read a short lesson, then do graded exercises on a live machine in the browser. You can fail on that machine without taking a production system down.

A track is one engineering discipline: readings beside the work, a real environment, instant grading, and community access. It costs ₹250 to ₹500 per track per month. The price depends on the infrastructure that track needs.

A project is a longer staged build that ends in a working system. An LSM tree, a B+ tree storage engine, Raft, and a small SQL engine cost ₹500 per year each. A key-value store called Cinder costs ₹750 per year.

You do not need your own cloud account. No opening discount is offered. Paper of the Month, short questions on one landmark paper, is on the site and has no price.
